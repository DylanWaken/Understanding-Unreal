## SH convention and response units

The stored SH is the sphere-average radiance projection used by `ScreenProbeConvertToIrradiance`: L0 in `ScreenProbeRadianceSHAmbient`, coefficients 1..8 in six RGBA16F texel columns per probe in `ScreenProbeRadianceSHDirectional`.  Coefficient order is `L0, L1_y, L1_z, L1_x, L2_xy, L2_yz, L2_z, L2_xz, L2_xy2`; every flat 27-value array in the data is R0..R8, G0..G8, B0..B8.

The signed unit-albedo Lambert response is

`D(n) = 4 * sum_i sh_i * CalcDiffuseTransferSH3(n)_i`

with AO=1 and exponent=1.  This is the shader's `EvaluateSHIrradiance` without its display zero-clamp and with the factor 4 that maps a constant radiance field back to its radiance.  `D` is still view-pre-exposed.  The physical irradiance forms are `pi*D` (the shader's `4*pi*DotSH3`) and `pi*D/PreExposure` (un-preexposed), where `PreExposure` is read from the captured View uniform buffer.

Constant-radiance self-test: projected L0 = `0.282095000` (expected `0.282095000`), max other coefficient = `1.047e-02`, exact-basis max |D-1| = `1.476e-06`, captured-8x8-quadrature max |D-1| = `2.051e-02` over 256 normals.  Normalization: **PASS**.

The exact-basis test is the normalization check.  The captured 8x8 equi-area projection leaves a small higher-band residual (`max other coefficient` above); that is the shader's discrete quadrature, not a decoder error.

## Lobes and figures

The projected lobes are 3-D response surfaces: for each of 162 hypothetical receiver normals the signed RGB response is evaluated, negative display response is explicitly clamped to zero, and the vertex radius is `60.0 cm * clamp(L / 1.5239, 0, 1)` in world space.  The same world radius and luminance scale are used for every state and every focused probe; nothing is normalized per probe.  Vertices are projected with the captured view and depth-tested against the captured `SceneDepthZ`; visible quads are filled and depth-occluded quads are drawn as an explicit magenta x-ray function glyph (not a physical object).  The negative signed luminance fraction is `0.0358`; the display-saturated fraction is `0.0097`.

Figures have no burned-in text; the HTML collector adds the labels:

## Active vs stale

Only adaptive indices below the captured allocator bound are active.  Finite storage beyond that bound is recorded as `stale` and its SH is not decoded.  Uniform probes with negative scene depth are recorded as `inactive_unlit`; uniform cells with zero depth are counted but not listed.  No shader-local adaptive-index assignment table, blend weight or ray weight is claimed: the per-index atlas position is the captured `AdaptiveScreenProbeData` word and the SH is the captured texture value at the corresponding atlas coordinate.

## Reuse

`python temporal-sh/decoder.py --dump-root <run-dir> --states baseline,lightstep-... --out <out-dir> --stage all`

`--stage data` writes `publish/data`; `--stage figures` re-renders figures from the decoded data.  `--state NAME=PATH` accepts an explicit selected root.  The JSON/NPZ schema is stable for the main temporal collector: states, active probes, signed SH, view matrices/exposure, focused matches/deltas and lobe arrays.
